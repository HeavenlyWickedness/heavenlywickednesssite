Frequently Asked Questions

Where does my donation go?

Donations go directly toward completing and releasing Heavenly Wickedness, including music, sound design, color, visual effects, festival submissions, and screenings.

Follow our socials, share the website and Heavenly Signature store, or simply help spread the word about Heavenly Wickedness. If you’d like to get more involved, email us at heavenlywickedness@gmail.com.

Can I donate another way?

Yes. In addition to online donations, we can accept contributions by check, ACH or wire transfer, stock transfer, and cryptocurrency. Email heavenlywickedness@gmail.com for instructions.
